DETERMINISTIC COST

Fixed kernels, fixed window, one linear head. The latency you measure here is the same operation the flight runtime performs, and its single core benchmark from the NPPAD-Edge project is on the ML page.

KNOWN LIMITS, ON THE RECORD

The classifier knows 12 accident classes and nothing else. The Normal Operation and Pre Onset scenarios show what it does with data outside that set: it still picks a class. Mission rules must carry an onset detector and an out of set guard in front of it. Confidence is the softmax of the head scores: indicative, not calibrated.
