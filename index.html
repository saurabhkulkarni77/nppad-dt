WHY THE TWIN RIDES ALONG

Engineers use digital twin technology to simulate and verify behaviour before committing commands to a real system. This twin does the same job for a reactor in deep space: it replays genuine accident transients against the flight diagnosis model, so the model's behaviour on LOCA, steam generator tube rupture and the rest of the accident set is known before the hardware ever flies.

The twin and the flight console share one model and one set of computed results. What the twin shows is what the spacecraft would decide.
