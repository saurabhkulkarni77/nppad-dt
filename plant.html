THE PLANT: A PRESSURIZED WATER REACTOR, LIVE FROM RECORDED DATA

PCTRAN, the simulator behind the NPPAD dataset, models a pressurized water reactor. This schematic shows that plant: containment, reactor pressure vessel and core, pressurizer, steam generator, reactor coolant pump and the primary loop, then the secondary loop with turbine, generator and condenser. Pick any scenario the Space App console uses and press play. Every readout on the drawing is a recorded channel value from that scenario's run at the time on the clock, taken from the same recorded data the rest of the twin ships.

RUN CLOCK 0 s

Series are shown at 20 s steps (every second recorded sample); the values are the recorded samples themselves, never interpolated. The generator and the condenser carry no live value: the dataset records no process channel for them, so they are labeled without one rather than given an invented number.

Recorded predictions at the twin protocol: onset aligned at t0 = 100 s, W = 60 s, realistic channels, copied from the scenario data the Space App console ships. CNN and GRU were saved by the study only as aggregate scores, so no recorded per window prediction exists for them.
